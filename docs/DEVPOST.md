# Pick the Rare submission notes

**Category:** Best Daily Game, Reddit’s Game of the Day Hackathon.

Pick the answer you expect the fewest players to choose. Predict the most popular answer for an optional bonus. Your choice is sealed until the midnight UTC cutoff. After finalization, see how the crowd answered, check your score and return for the next daily question. Community submissions and a moderator-reviewed ballot feed future rounds.
